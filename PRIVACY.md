# Privacy policy

Microphone plays whatever your phone's microphone hears through its audio output, live.

- Audio from the microphone goes straight to the speaker or headphones. It is never recorded,
  stored or sent anywhere.
- The app has no internet permission, so it can't send data off your device.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The only thing it saves is a note of which version you last opened, so it knows when to show
  the "what's new" dialog. That stays on your device.

Permissions:

- **Microphone**: needed to hear you.
- **Notifications** (optional): shows a notification while the mic is on, which you can tap to
  turn it off.
