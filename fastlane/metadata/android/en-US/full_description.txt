Microphone turns your phone into a live microphone. Speak into it and your voice comes straight out of the speaker, your headphones or a Bluetooth speaker.

Ideas:
• Make a toy talk: hide a Bluetooth speaker inside it and speak into the phone
• Talk to the next room through a Bluetooth speaker, like a walkie-talkie
• Use it as a simple PA for a party, a class or a show
• Hear yourself in headphones while you practise singing or speaking
• Use a Bluetooth headset as the mic and hear it on the phone's speaker

Features:
• Choose the microphone: phone, wired headset, Bluetooth or USB
• Choose where the sound goes: phone speaker, headphones, Bluetooth or USB
• Keeps working with the screen off
• Stop it any time from the notification

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The app has no internet access, so your voice never leaves the phone.

Tip: the phone's own mic and speaker together will squeal with feedback. Use headphones, or a speaker a little way from the phone.

Open source: https://github.com/bitplane/Microphone
